Colour

Four brand colours. Everything else is evidence.

Keep the page 80–90% white or pale grey, 5–15% deep navy, and under 5% electric blue. Supporting blues exist for charts, not decoration.

Spacing

A 4px grid is the whole layout system.

Allowed values: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Page gutters start at 48–64px. Sections break at 64–96px. Never invent 17 or 27.

Article

Most digital programs stall because they optimise activity, not decisions.

The organisations that pull ahead treat technology as a way to change a short list of choices, not as a catalogue of tools. That sounds obvious. It is rarely how the work is run.

A typical transformation still starts with platforms, vendors, and a slide of “use cases.” Eighteen months later the stack is richer and the P&L has not moved. The missing step is editorial: name the decisions that create value, then build only what those decisions need.

That discipline shows up in the design language as well. Headlines state the finding. Body type stays legible at 150% line-height. Charts argue with one highlighted series. Nothing is boxed for decoration.

What to do in the next two quarters

Pick five recurring decisions. Assign a single owner. Give that owner a complete view of the data, and stop funding work that does not change those five. The rest of the roadmap can wait.
